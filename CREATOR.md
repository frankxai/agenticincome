# Agentic Income Voice Cache

## Voice attributes

1. **Human-sovereign** — agents expand capability while an accountable person or legal entity remains principal.
2. **Composed conviction** — direct, ambitious, and calm; never guru-like, domineering, or breathless.
3. **Evidence-led** — mechanism, source, freshness, authority, and limitations appear near the claim.
4. **Commercial clarity** — speaks in buyers, accepted outcomes, scope, economics, support, and ownership.
5. **Technically generous** — gives humans and agents enough public structure to inspect, reuse, and challenge the method.

## Primary audience

AI-native founders, experts, consultants, creator-operators, and boutique teams with one valuable workflow, credible buyer access, domain judgment, or an existing audience. They want implementation and leverage without surrendering the customer relationship, accounts, repository, or IP.

## Content pillars

- Outcome Packs and principal-owned agent services.
- Governed workflows, authority boundaries, evals, and evidence.
- Offer design, implementation, delivery, and operating economics.
- Open-source contracts, reference systems, and machine-readable proof.
- Reader-first research with dates, alternatives, and disclosed incentives.

## Channel tone

- **Homepage:** felt customer problem, crisp category, one action, visible proof.
- **Product and offer pages:** precise scope, deliverables, acceptance, support, and access state.
- **Technical docs:** explicit contracts, schemas, modes, permissions, failure paths, and examples.
- **Research:** answer first, evidence date, tradeoffs, unpaid alternatives, disclosure before route.
- **Social:** useful observation plus concrete mechanism; no vague futurism or manufactured urgency.

## Terminology

Prefer: Outcome Pack, principal-owned, governed agent service, accepted outcome, authority boundary, evidence, runbook, scope-first, human gate.

Avoid in first-read public copy: Trinity, Explorer-to-Shaper, machines, command, autonomous income, passive-income guarantee, agent marketplace, superintelligence. Internal language may appear later only when it helps the user make a decision.

## Claim rule

Every material claim needs at least one of: an inspectable mechanism, public source, evidence date, acceptance check, explicit assumption, or clear limitation. A reference implementation is never described as customer proof.
