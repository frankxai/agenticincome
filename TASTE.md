# AgenticIncome Taste

## Keep

- Direct founder-to-founder language.
- Real repository and delivery proof.
- Calm density, precise borders, strong type, and quiet space.
- Explicit access states and honest commercial boundaries.
- One purposeful motion sequence with a complete reduced-motion state.

## Cut

- Passive-income fantasy, payout-first framing, and invented certainty.
- Internal operating language on customer surfaces.
- Generic AI gradients, decorative node clouds, fake dashboards, and filler card grids.
- Affiliate programs presented as the brand's primary capability.
- 3D or video without a product metaphor, fallback, provenance, and performance budget.

If an effect competes with scope, proof, or the next action, remove it. If a claim cannot link to an artifact or explain its mechanism, rewrite it.
