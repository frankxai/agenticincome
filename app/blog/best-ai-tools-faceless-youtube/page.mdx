## Where to actually spend (and where not to)

**Voice is the whole game.** Viewers forgive mediocre footage over a great voice; they will not forgive a robotic voice over great footage. This is the one line item to max out. <AffiliateLink tool="ElevenLabs" /> is still the quality leader, and the recurring affiliate terms mean if you recommend it after genuinely using it, the recommendation compounds. (If budget is tight, the [ElevenLabs alternatives](/blog/elevenlabs-alternatives) breakdown covers the cheaper voices that still pass.)

**Editing should be boring and cheap.** <AffiliateLink tool="CapCut" /> does 90% of what a faceless channel needs — cuts, captions, transitions, auto-captioning — and has a free plan. Don't buy Premiere for this.

**Repurposing is the hidden multiplier.** One 10-minute video is also 5 Shorts, a blog post, and an email. <AffiliateLink tool="Descript" /> lets you cut all of them from the same transcript. That's not a nice-to-have — it's how one upload becomes a week of content across the [network](/).

**Visuals: stock first, AI second.** Most faceless niches (finance, history, self-improvement) run fine on stock + motion graphics. Reach for <AffiliateLink tool="Higgsfield" /> when you need a specific shot that doesn't exist — it's the most controllable AI-motion tool and the one I actually use.

## A lean build

1. **Script** — frontier LLM free tier. Pays nothing, ranks everything. Use it, don't pay for it here.
2. **Voice** — ElevenLabs Creator, $22/mo. Non-negotiable.
3. **Edit + caption** — CapCut, free plan to start.
4. **Repurpose** — Descript Hobbyist, $16/mo billed annually or $24/mo billed monthly (add once you're publishing weekly).
5. **Thumbnail** — Canva free.

That's $22/mo to start and $38–46/mo once you add repurposing, as of October 2026. AI b-roll from Higgsfield Starter ($19/mo) is optional on top. If you later write your own comparison, ElevenLabs, CapCut and Higgsfield have recurring affiliate programs (Descript pays a one-time flat fee, and Canva's program is closed).
