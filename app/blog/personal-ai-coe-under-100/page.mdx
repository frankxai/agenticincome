## Pillar by pillar

**1. Reasoning** — one frontier LLM subscription. This is your analyst, editor, and strategist. Don't pay for three; pick one and go deep.

**2. Automation** — a coding agent (Claude Code, Cursor, or similar). This is the pillar most people skip and the one that actually compounds: it builds the scripts, sites, and pipelines that take over repetitive work. The whole [agentic-income network](/) is built this way.

**3. Creation (voice)** — <AffiliateLink tool="ElevenLabs" />. Text becomes narration, narration becomes video and podcast.

**4. Creation (visual + video)** — <AffiliateLink tool="Higgsfield" /> for AI motion, <AffiliateLink tool="CapCut" /> for assembly. Between them you cover every visual a content business needs.

**5. Distribution** — <AffiliateLink tool="Systeme.io" />. Email list, landing pages, and checkout in one free-to-start tool. The owned-audience pillar most creators bolt on too late. Its affiliate program pays 60% lifetime recurring, the highest recurring rate in our catalog.

**6. Measurement** — free analytics (your platform's native dashboards + a weekly review). You don't need a paid tool here yet; you need the habit.
