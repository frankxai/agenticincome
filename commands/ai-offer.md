# /ai-offer — Offer Design

Requires `income/01-income-architecture.md`. Read it plus `knowledge/03-agent-fulfillment.md` (patterns) and the **pricing-architecture** + **agent-fulfillment-design** skills.

## Process

1. **Offer spec.** Name, one-sentence promise (capability-framed, never income-framed), deliverables list, what's explicitly NOT included (scope armor), timeframe.
2. **Pricing.** Apply pricing-architecture skill: outcome-anchored for services (never hourly — agents collapsed your hours; keep the surplus), $19–$149 self-serve band for products, honest maintenance-cost framing for systems. One price, one optional premium tier max. No fake anchors, no countdown timers.
3. **Fulfillment design (the differentiator).** Design the agent team that delivers: which pattern (session/team/loop/pipeline), the brief template buyers/clients fill, the delivery checklist, verification stage, handover artifact. This is where "agents lead delivery" becomes real — write the actual subagent roles and their quality gates.
4. **Buyer journey.** Discovery → landing → purchase (MoR link) → onboarding (self-serve, zero-call) → delivery → handover + review ask. Every step self-service unless this is a deliberate apex service.
5. **Objection surface.** The three real objections and the honest answer to each (if an objection has no honest answer, the offer needs redesign, not better copy).

## Artifact

Write `income/02-offer.md` with all sections, ending with the **fulfillment runbook** (step-by-step, agent roles inline) and "Next: `/ai ship`."

Doctrine: the promise must be deliverable on your worst week; price the outcome; a confused buyer costs more than a lost one — clarity beats cleverness everywhere.
