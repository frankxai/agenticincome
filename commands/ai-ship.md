# /ai-ship — Launch

Requires `income/02-offer.md`. Read it plus `knowledge/04-tool-stack.md` (stack slots) and `knowledge/05-honest-marketing.md` (voice + disclosure rules — these are hard constraints on all copy you write here).

## Process

1. **Landing page.** Draft the full page copy: capability-framed hero, the mechanism section (show the machinery — it's the differentiator), honest-costs block, offer spec, FAQ from the objection surface, single CTA. If they have a site repo, build the page; if not, scaffold one (Next.js or their stack) or configure the MoR-hosted page as the interim.
2. **Checkout.** Walk MoR setup concretely: product creation, personal-license terms for digital goods, EU VAT handled by MoR, test purchase, receipt email copy. No custom Stripe integration at this stage — that's deliberate.
3. **Delivery wiring.** Connect purchase → fulfillment: for products, the download/repo-access flow; for services, the intake brief → agent-team kickoff from the fulfillment runbook.
4. **Launch sequence (earned channels only).** Announcement post per existing surface in its native format; the build-in-public artifact (what you made and why — machinery, not money); newsletter send if a list exists. No paid ads, no launch-bro countdowns.
5. **Measurement.** Wire the analytics triangle (knowledge/04 slot 4) + define the ship-week numbers that matter: visits, checkout starts, purchases, refunds, one qualitative signal (replies/questions).

## Artifact

Write `income/03-launch-runbook.md`: pre-flight checklist (every gate above with status), launch-day sequence with the actual post drafts inline, measurement plan, and the first `/ai operate` date booked one week out.

Doctrine: ship the modest version this week over the impressive version next month; every claim on the landing page must trace to something real; disclosure inline wherever a compensated link appears.
