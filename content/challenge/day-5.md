Your node is live. Today it stops being just a page and starts being an asset you own.

**Today's job: add exactly one email capture to your node.**

Keep the promise specific and tied to the exact thing your reader came for. Vague "join my newsletter" language converts badly and honestly deserves to. Better shapes:

- "Get the updated [category] shortlist when it changes."
- "Get the exact stack checklist for this workflow."
- "Get notified if the recommendation changes."

Whatever free tool you published on (Systeme.io, Notion, etc.) almost certainly has a built-in form for this — you don't need a separate email tool yet. The point isn't sophistication, it's that a stranger's visit turns into a relationship you can reach again, instead of a click that vanishes the moment they close the tab.

**Why this is worth a whole day of the challenge:** affiliate clicks fund the floor, but an owned list is the compounding asset underneath the whole system. This network's own homepage capture ("Get the next payer before the crowd") exists for exactly this reason — a payout can disappear when a program changes terms; a list you own does not.

**Do this before tomorrow:** one working email capture, live on your node, with a specific promise attached.
