Everything so far has been building toward today: proof the machine is real.

**Today's job: share your node with three real people who might genuinely want it**, and get at least one honest click through your affiliate link.

Three people is a deliberately small, doable number:

- A friend or colleague who's actually mentioned the problem your node solves.
- A relevant community, subreddit, or group — shared honestly, not spammed.
- One post on whatever platform you already use, written like you'd write to a person, not an ad.

**How to know it actually worked:** log into the affiliate dashboard for the program you joined on Day 2 and check for a real click or referral event tied to your link. Most dashboards show this within minutes to hours. That number — one, real, verifiable — is worth more than any screenshot of hypothetical earnings, because you can trace exactly where it came from.

**If you get zero clicks today:** that's a true, useful data point, not a failure. It tells you something real about distribution, not about whether the system works. Three people is a small sample on purpose — day 7 is about reading whatever number you actually got, honestly.

**Do this before tomorrow:** the node shared with three real people, and a check of your affiliate dashboard for any tracked activity.
