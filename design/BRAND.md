# Agentic Income Brand

## Category

Agentic Income is the open field guide and implementation studio for
principal-owned Outcome Packs: governed agent services built around one buyer,
one accepted outcome, and assets the principal keeps.

## Positioning

For founders, experts, consultants, creator-operators, and boutique teams who
understand a valuable workflow, Agentic Income turns that judgment into a
governed agent service. Unlike generic AI courses, no-code automation agencies,
or passive-income promises, the method is public, the scope is testable, the
authority boundary is explicit, and the customer relationship, accounts,
repository, and IP remain with the principal.

## Personality

- Human-sovereign: agents expand capability; a person or legal entity owns and approves.
- Technically generous: useful public contracts and examples come before any ask.
- Composed: high conviction, low theatricality.
- Commercially exact: buyer, outcome, acceptance, authority, economics, and ownership are named.
- Evidence-led: source type, date, mechanism, and limitation sit near the claim.

## Promise

Turn one valuable workflow into an income system you own.

Supporting line: Own the outcome.

## Transformation

Move the visitor from undirected AI ambition to one testable Outcome Pack:

1. They name a buyer and a costly job.
2. They define an accepted outcome and stop conditions.
3. They map the governed workflow and human approval boundary.
4. They inspect the public schema, scaffold, validator, tests, and reference desk.
5. They leave with a useful map, join the selective Lab, or request a scoped build.

## Voice

- Short, natural sentences with calm authority.
- Operational nouns: buyer, outcome, scope, acceptance, principal, workflow, authority, eval, runbook, evidence.
- Claims must be backed by a mechanism, a tool, a route, or a disclosure.
- Prefer “here is the contract” over “imagine the future.”

## Visual world

- Near-black ink field; warm ivory type; emerald capability; cyan system;
  restrained amber authority.
- Geist and Geist Mono for product truth; Bricolage only for decisive display.
- Quiet architectural grids, ledgers, contracts, source rails, and material depth.
- One idea per viewport and 40–60% quiet space on major compositions.

## Avoid

- Get-rich-quick framing.
- Passive-income fantasy language.
- Labour-erasure language such as “the hours are over.”
- Internal queue, swarm, harness, operator, tier, or control-plane language.
- Generic AI gradients, glowing blobs, or fake dashboards.
- Multiple equal-weight offers above the fold.
- Fake waitlist counts, capacity, urgency, customers, or outcomes.
- Copy that hides an affiliate incentive or calls vendor evidence customer proof.
- Generated graphics with broken text or unreadable UI.
