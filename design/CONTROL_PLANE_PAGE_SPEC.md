# AgenticIncome Control-Plane Route Spec — 2026-07-13

## Audience

Founders and domain experts with one valuable customer job who need to understand how a supervised agent system is built, operated, and proven before requesting implementation.

## Route jobs

- `/build`: define the Outcome Pack and move from request to verified preview.
- `/build/request-to-quote`: produce a browser-local scope artifact without submitting data, pricing work, or creating an agreement.
- `/operate`: explain the recurring operating loop and the three authority lanes.
- `/proof`: separate system, delivery, and settled economic evidence.

## Conversion

Primary: use the Request-to-Quote reference desk or inspect the scoped Implementation Sprint. Secondary: install the `/ai` pack and inspect public system evidence.

## Language boundaries

- No autonomous-business, income-guarantee, customer-result, or current-community milestone claims.
- Agents may prepare drafts, tests, evidence, and internal decisions; an authorized principal owns contracts, pricing, customer sends, credentials, production promotion, and money movement.
- The Request-to-Quote desk is browser-local and explicitly not a connected intake or pricing engine.

## Responsive and accessibility contract

Server Components are the default. The local desk is the only Client Component. Every field has a label, output uses `aria-live`, forms work by keyboard, content reflows to one column, and no critical meaning depends on motion.
