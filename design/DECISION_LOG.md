# Product and Design Decision Log

## 2026-07-21 — Consolidate the public category around Outcome Packs

**Decision:** Position Agentic Income as the home of principal-owned agent services and Outcome Pack implementation.

**Why:** The repo’s strongest differentiated artifacts are the public operating contract, authority model, Request-to-Quote reference system, build path, and Git-backed delivery method. AI-tool directories and generic agent marketplaces are crowded categories; affiliate research is valuable distribution but creates a weaker first-read identity.

**Consequences:** The homepage leads with the owned workflow outcome, `/start` becomes the capability-map funnel, the public skill becomes machine-readable proof, and implementation is the commercial core. Research remains accessible but secondary.

## 2026-07-21 — Preserve human principal authority

**Decision:** Replace domination and labor-erasure language with explicit human-agent complementarity.

**Why:** Trustworthy agent commerce requires a legal or human principal for contracts, accounts, credentials, customer sends, production promotion, public claims, and money movement.

**Consequences:** Public copy names what agents may prepare and what requires authorized approval. “The hours are over,” “the machines are yours,” and “a team you command” are retired from first-read brand language.

## 2026-07-21 — Use coded proof instead of a new generated hero

**Decision:** Ship the responsive Outcome Pack desk as the signature hero visual.

**Why:** It explains the system, is accessible, adapts to mobile and reduced motion, and creates stronger trust than decorative media. Existing generated media can be reconsidered after provenance and publication records are complete.
