# Agentic Income Design System

## Visual Idea

The Income Desk: a dark, precise command surface where comparison evidence, recurring-payer status, and audience capture are visible as one compounding system.

## Signature Scene

Chaos becomes a routed desk. Fragmented panels, loose signals, and unowned search demand converge into a clean operating table, recurring ledger, and owned-audience vault. The scene can be generated as a cinematic poster, but the labels, claims, and product logic must be coded.

## Layout Rules

- First viewport must show the product mechanism, not only a mood image.
- Use glass only for command decks, proof chips, tables, and media frames.
- Body text lives on solid or near-solid surfaces.
- No cards inside cards unless the inner object is a real tool surface.
- Diagrams with exact text are coded in SVG/HTML, not generated as raster images.

## Logo Rules

- The canonical mark is vector-first.
- The mark must work at 16px, 32px, one-color, nav, favicon, and social avatar.
- Rendered or 3D variants are applications, never the identity source.

## Components

- Income desk hero: comparison engine, payer ledger, audience capture.
- Transformation poster: generated, inspected, optimized, and paired with coded before/after copy.
- Flywheel diagram: coded, readable, exact labels.
- Tool rows: real commission, recurring status, action link only when available.
- Offers: direct product ladder with clear disclosure and no fake scarcity.
- Design route: public proof of operating taste and QA discipline.
