# Homepage Positioning Page Spec

## Purpose

Make a qualified visitor understand the category, see the ownership boundary, inspect real proof, and choose the right next action without decoding internal ecosystem language.

## First read

“Agentic Income turns one valuable workflow into a governed income system I own. Agents do meaningful execution; I retain judgment, accountability, customer trust, accounts, repository, and IP.”

## Primary audience

Founder, domain expert, consultant, creator-operator, or boutique team with a credible workflow and buyer access. Secondary audiences are independent builders inspecting the open method and agents looking for explicit contracts.

## Main product and hierarchy

1. Outcome Pack implementation sprint — commercial core.
2. Free capability map and public operating skill — primary entry.
3. Reference systems, templates, and self-serve offers — proof and lower-commitment adoption.
4. Dated editorial and affiliate research — distribution layer.

## Primary conversion

Homepage primary CTA: **Map my first system** → `/start`.

Secondary CTA: **Inspect the public method** → public `agentic-income-skills` contract.

Qualified implementation CTA appears after the method and authority model are understood. No email gate is used as the primary funnel until owned capture is connected and verified.

## Narrative order

1. Hero: buyer, owned outcome, and Outcome Pack signature visual.
2. Business architecture: implementation, self-serve, open proof, research.
3. Human + agent relationship: signal, execution, retained assets.
4. Workflow proof: Request-to-Quote reference system and human gate.
5. Research: secondary, dated, disclosed.
6. Conversion: capability map or scope-first implementation.

## Trust requirements

- Only link public repositories from public trust claims.
- No income, speed, customer, scarcity, or capacity claim without evidence.
- No checkout or capture success claim when the external service is unwired.
- Reference implementations are labeled as reference implementations.
- Production actions, customer sends, contracts, credentials, and money remain principal-gated.

## Responsive hierarchy

Desktop may show copy and Outcome Pack desk side by side. Mobile stacks copy, CTA, authority note, desk, and proof rail in that order. Comparison grids collapse to one column. No essential meaning depends on motion, hover, or horizontal scrolling.

## Accessibility and motion

Semantic headings, visible focus, keyboard-safe controls, target sizes of at least 44px where practical, contrast at AA or better, reduced-motion support, and no auto-playing media. Motion reveals hierarchy; it never carries unique information.
