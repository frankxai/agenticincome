# Homepage Positioning Scene Brief

## Scene concept

**The Principal’s Outcome Desk** — a calm editorial workspace where one valuable workflow becomes an accepted, governed service.

## Composition

- Left: human-facing promise, ideal audience, primary and secondary actions, authority note.
- Right: coded Outcome Pack desk with Discover → Validate → Build → Prove.
- Lower rail: public contract, reference system, deployable template, and live routing proof.

The visual center of gravity is the Outcome Pack, not a robot, dashboard collage, coin, or abstract AI aura.

## Materials and color

- Deep ink and graphite surfaces for seriousness and continuity.
- Warm ivory for readable human language.
- Cyan as system signal and interaction state.
- Restrained gold for ownership, value, and authority gates.
- Hairline borders, quiet depth, and generous negative space.

## Motion

Static composition first. On capable devices, reveal the four stages in sequence and use subtle panel parallax only if it remains smooth. Reduced-motion mode presents the complete scene immediately. No looping spectacle.

## Image-generation decision

Do not generate a new hero asset for this release. Existing media is visually strong but less precise than the coded proof surface, and several generated assets lack a complete publication/provenance packet. Reconsider generated editorial media only for a later human transformation story, with a VIS provenance record and an explicit fallback.

## Failure conditions

- Hero reads like a generic AI agency or passive-income pitch.
- Internal pack names appear before the customer outcome.
- Agent execution is framed as eliminating human judgment or accountability.
- Decorative media attracts more attention than the offer and proof.
- Public proof links resolve to private or inaccessible repositories.
