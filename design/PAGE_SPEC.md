# Homepage Page Spec — 2026-07-29

## Audience

AI-native founders, experts, consultants, creator-operators, and boutique teams
with one valuable workflow, credible buyer access, domain judgment, or an
existing audience. They want useful leverage without surrendering the customer
relationship, accounts, repository, or IP.

## Conversion

Primary: start the free Agentic Income Field Guide. Secondary: inspect and run
the public system. Qualified visitors may join the selective Outcome Pack Lab
waitlist or inspect a scope-first Outcome Pack Build Sprint. There is no payment
or email gate in the first-use path.

## Scene Order

1. Buyer-first category and promise, free CTA, public-source CTA, four-stage Outcome Pack desk, proof rail.
2. Free value: a 30-minute opportunity map, three paths, six-part Outcome Pack anatomy, and executable public commands.
3. Sourced market evidence labelled by source type and explicitly separated from Agentic Income customer proof.
4. Outcome Pack anatomy and the human-principal / agent-capability boundary.
5. Three honest next steps: free Field Guide, selective Lab waitlist, scope-first Build Sprint.
6. One inspectable workflow with exact command, source links, output artifact, and human publish gate.
7. Dated, disclosed research as a secondary layer.
8. Final free-first conversion.

## Offer architecture

- **Agentic Income Field Guide — free and live:** buyer map, path selection,
  Outcome Pack anatomy, public skill, schema, reference desk, and evidence rules.
- **Outcome Pack Lab — coming soon:** selective founding cohort for people who
  can bring one real workflow and judge the result. The waitlist must never
  imply a request was retained when intake is not connected.
- **Outcome Pack Build Sprint — scope-first:** one buyer, one accepted outcome,
  one governed workflow, one owned repository, one verification bundle, and one
  operating handoff. No checkout before scope and acceptance are defined.

## Three design directions

1. **Cinematic Machine — rejected.** The 340vh canvas hero is technically
   impressive but abstract, client-heavy, and centred on labour-erasure language.
2. **Human Studio — supporting influence.** Warmer editorial storytelling is
   useful for voice and examples, but as the primary direction it risks a generic
   creator-course feel and underplays executable proof.
3. **Principal's Outcome Desk — selected.** Evidence-led technical editorial:
   one human outcome, a coded Outcome Pack desk, quiet material depth, sparse
   proof rails, and a single free-first action.

## Public install contract

- Every executable install command must run against `https://github.com/frankxai/agentic-income-skills`.
- Public source and MIT links must resolve to that public repository or one of
  its files.
- The public command path is the repository's actual package surface:
  `income:init`, `income:validate`, and `test`.
- Do not advertise a Claude marketplace, plugin command, or one-command install
  that depends on the private application repository.
- Do not link the private application repository from `app/`, `components/`, or
  `lib/`.

## Interaction and quality contract

- One primary CTA per viewport; secondary actions are visually subordinate.
- No document or navigation overflow at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440px.
- All form inputs have visible labels, 44px minimum targets, explicit loading,
  success, unavailable, and recovery states, plus polite live announcements.
- Static content is complete without JavaScript. Motion uses only opacity and
  transform, explains hierarchy or state, and collapses under reduced motion.
- No generated hero media, WebGL, fake dashboard, fake waitlist count, customer
  claim, or income claim. The coded product model is the primary visual.
- Source labels distinguish platform data, vendor-published customer stories,
  public system evidence, delivery evidence, and settled economic evidence.
