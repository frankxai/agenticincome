# Agentic Income Taste

## Keep

- Calm density.
- Sharp hierarchy.
- Real table data.
- Coded proof diagrams.
- Small, useful motion.
- Clear disclosure.

## Cut

- Generic "AI-powered" copy.
- Purple-blue gradient sameness without contrast.
- Broken generated text.
- Hero media that hides the product.
- Motion on every section.
- Any CTA that implies a payout is guaranteed.

## First-Read Test

Within three seconds, a visitor should understand:

- This is Agentic Income.
- It is for building recurring income around AI tools.
- The mechanism is honest comparisons plus recurring-payer routing plus owned audience.
- The next action is start, compare, inspect tools, or view offers.
- The trust proof is the visible catalog, disclosure, and operating system.

## Design Council Verdict

- Creative direction: "private income intelligence desk."
- Product direction: first viewport shows the operating mechanism.
- Motion direction: one compounding flow trace, all else quiet.
- Visual QA direction: coded assets replace generated images when exact text matters.
