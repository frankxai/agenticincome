The paid difference must be substantial: a finished executable customer workflow, real vertical examples, meaningful failure evals, exportability, clear compatibility, installation diagnostics, recovery and sustainable updates. Packaging a free sample behind a price is not sufficient. Use the customer's machine and keys when a model is required, and disclose external provider costs and terms.

Before checkout: exact-offer merchant/account eligibility; owned/licensed input inventory; accepted price/currency/tax/consumer terms; signed provider webhook and payment-status recheck; durable idempotent order/entitlement; authorized private delivery; duplicate/delayed/declined/replay recovery; refund/revocation; support/privacy/security; independent review and exact preview. A redirect or configured link grants no access and proves no payment.

## Blockchain earns a bounded role

Off-chain entitlement is the product authority. First prove normal payment, delivery and recovery. Next consider a signed portable receipt only if customers need independent provenance; keep personal information and prompts off-chain. For a licensed research API, x402 may later meter one request under explicit human spending limits and server verification/settlement, but a payment protocol does not establish data rights or refund policy.

[Stripe stablecoin documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/stablecoin-payments) checked 11 October describes EU businesses, including the Netherlands, as private-preview eligible geography. It is not account acceptance. It describes local-currency settlement and refunds to the original wallet. Keep this route proposed until actual account capability, merchant mode, settlement, accounting, consumer rights and security approval exist. [Coinbase x402 overview](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/welcome) documents payment-gated API access; no wallet, signed transaction or paid call is made here. No token sale, investment claim or personal-data resale belongs in this launch.

## Growth experiment that can be measured

Use one sequential experiment before a split test: show the worked example first, then the planner first, within fixed declared windows and unchanged qualified distribution sources. Do not conclude a winner from downloads, staff checks or unequal exposure.

The future first-party event contract needs minimal fields: experiment/version, variant, pseudonymous consented session, source category and event time. `eligible_start` means an eligible visitor begins the declared workflow; `accepted_output` means explicit acceptance of that output, linked to its artifact version without sending input text. Exclude bots, staff, fixture runs, duplicate sessions and tests; record exclusions, window and source. Denominator is unique eligible starts; numerator is unique accepted outputs from that cohort. Unknown collection remains unknown. Validation/receipt generation itself is not acceptance.

Paid conversion comes only from accepted server-side provider events, separate from free activation. Reconcile gross collections − refunds − provider fees; then subtract attributable fulfilment/model/support costs for contribution. Owner time and costs without evidence stay unknown. No tracker or collection endpoint is described as enabled by this document.
