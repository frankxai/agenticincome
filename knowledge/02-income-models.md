# The three income models (and the one path through them)

Every legitimate way a solo creator converts agentic capability into revenue reduces to three models. They are not alternatives — they are **stages**. Almost everyone should walk them in order, because each stage funds and de-risks the next.

## Model 1 — Services (agent-amplified labor)

**Mechanism:** you sell an outcome to a specific client; agents compress your delivery cost.

- **Examples that work in 2026:** AI workflow implementation for small firms, content-system builds, site + funnel builds, data/report automation, "agent audits" of existing businesses.
- **Economics:** highest hourly yield of the three, fastest to first dollar (days, not months). A scoped implementation that took a 2019 freelancer three weeks is now a 2–4 day delivery with an agent team — but **price the outcome, not your hours**, or the compression benefits your client instead of you.
- **Honest costs:** still labor-coupled. Scope creep is the killer; agents make delivery cheap but discovery and trust expensive. Cap concurrent clients before quality collapses.
- **The trap:** staying here forever. Services are the funding stage, not the destination. Skim 20% of every service week into building Model 2 assets.

## Model 2 — Products (packaged judgment)

**Mechanism:** you package the *decisions* you made in service work — templates, packs, blueprints, kits — and sell them self-serve.

- **Examples:** agent packs (like the ones in this repo), template systems, business-plan kits, prompt/workflow libraries with real depth, niche datasets, micro-tools.
- **Economics:** price range that reliably clears self-serve without sales calls: **$19–$149**. Above that, buyers expect humans. After merchant-of-record fees you keep roughly 92–95% of each sale in that range on Polar's Starter rate of 5% + $0.50 (checked 2026-09-29 at [polar.sh/resources/pricing](https://polar.sh/resources/pricing); see [07](07-field-dossier.md) §3): a $19 sale pays $0.95 + $0.50 = $1.45 and nets $17.55 (92.4%), a $149 sale pays $7.45 + $0.50 = $7.95 and nets $141.05 (94.7%). International cards add 1.5% (about 90.9% at $19 and 93.2% at $149), and payout fees ($2/month plus 0.25% + $0.25 per payout), currency conversion (0.25–1%) and $15 per dispute come on top, before your own costs. Volume is the whole game, and volume = distribution (see [05](05-honest-marketing.md)).
- **Honest costs:** a product is a promise of maintenance. Stale products refund and reputation-burn. Budget the refresh loop before you launch (our packs automate theirs — copy that pattern).
- **The 2026 edge:** *agent-native products* — products that are themselves agents/skills/workflows the buyer installs — have structurally better retention than PDFs and courses, because they do work instead of assigning it.

## Model 3 — Systems (owned machines)

**Mechanism:** you own an asset that produces income with maintenance measured in hours per month — a publication, a product line with evergreen distribution, an affiliate property, a licensed catalog.

- This is the domain of the sibling properties: [agenticpassiveincome.com](https://agenticpassiveincome.com) teaches the stream mechanics (`/api` pack); [dpi](https://github.com/frankxai/dpi) (private repo as of 2026-09-28) governs the portfolio layer (`/dpi` pack).
- **Economics:** slowest to first dollar (weeks to months), highest ceiling, and the only model whose yield curve is decoupled from your calendar. The compounding asset is usually **distribution** (audience, SEO, syndication), not the content itself.
- **Honest costs:** "passive" means *structurally decoupled from hours*, not zero hours. Every real system has a maintenance floor — we publish it per-stream in the Stream Atlas instead of pretending otherwise.

## The path (what `/ai architect` actually computes)

```
Weeks 1–4     Model 1: one scoped service offer. Goal: first revenue + 2 delivery case artifacts.
Months 2–3    Model 2: package the delivery system you just used. Goal: first 10 self-serve sales.
Months 3–6    Model 3: pick ONE stream that compounds your existing surfaces. Goal: first system live with a weekly loop.
Ongoing       /dpi: track each asset in your ledger, gate-check quarterly, kill what stalls.
```

The ratios matter more than the calendar: **one** offer, **one** product, **one** stream. The portfolio comes later; focus is the multiplier at this stage.

## Choosing your entry (the honest selector)

| Your situation | Enter at | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Skills but no audience, need money in ≤30 days | Services | Only model with day-scale latency to cash |
| Existing audience or traffic surface (even small) | Products | Distribution is the hard part; you have a seed |
| Existing income, building for the decade | Systems + dpi | You can afford the latency for the decoupling |
