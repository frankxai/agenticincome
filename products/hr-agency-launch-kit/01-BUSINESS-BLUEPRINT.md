# The business blueprint

## The position

Build a specialist agency for a repeatable hiring and onboarding problem. Use AI to make research, preparation, coordination and documentation cheaper to deliver. Keep a named expert responsible for judgment and client relationships.

The first buyer hypothesis is an owner-led business with 20–150 employees, recurring specialist hiring and no substantial internal recruiting operations team. The service is a bounded hiring desk for one open role. For the digital product itself, the buyer is an experienced recruiter, boutique HR agency owner, or AI builder partnered with an experienced HR practitioner.

Do not market the kit as a substitute for professional recruiting competence. A builder contributes integration and evaluation; the practitioner owns the hiring method.

## Select the niche

Score three niches using evidence you can actually obtain: access to hiring managers, recurring demand, access to job-relevant evidence, repeatable delivery and cost of getting the assessment wrong. Avoid selecting a niche solely because salaries or placement fees appear high.

Worked hypothesis: small B2B software companies hiring customer-success managers. A founder has a role brief, a client-facing work sample and a hiring manager who can approve criteria. This makes the first workflow easier to evaluate than an undefined executive transformation offer.

Before pitching, record five actual examples of open roles and three buyer conversations with permission to retain notes. Ask what happened in the last hire, where work stalled, who did the coordination and what was purchased. No conversations have been conducted for this kit. Vendor marketing establishes available machinery, not willingness to pay.

## The offer

“An organized hiring process for one customer-success role: a calibrated brief, reviewed candidate evidence, interview coordination, decision records and an onboarding handoff.”

The client buys a named deliverable and service boundary. A signed scope specifies role, duration, candidate volume, channels, decision owner, acceptance, revisions and exclusions. Do not guarantee a hire, revenue, candidate quality or time-to-fill.

The first milestone is a hiring brief accepted by the hiring manager. The second is reviewed evidence prepared for a decision. The third is an accepted onboarding handoff after a human hiring decision. Invoicing follows contracted milestones, not raw AI activity.

## What the agency owns

Own your service definition, evaluation method, client relationship, templates and ability to change providers. The client owns its production accounts and records according to the agreement. Keep acquisition contacts, candidate information, company knowledge and original teaching in separate permission scopes.

Buy commodity infrastructure: ATS, scheduling, transcription, identity and accounting. Configure what already exists before writing another application. Custom engineering earns its place when a recurring handoff lacks a reliable native solution.

The founding team needs an HR decision owner, a delivery owner and an integration maintainer. One person may cover more than one role, but all three responsibilities must be explicit. Financial planning includes every person's hours.

## How the agency finds clients

Start with one channel: introductions and individually reviewed contact to hiring managers in the selected niche. Prepare an original one-page diagnosis using the company's public role information and a concrete sample deliverable.

Keep client development separate from candidate outreach. A prospect database is not permission to contact every person in it. Choose allowed sources and channels, honor opt-outs, approve the exact recipient set and message, and retain the relevant send record.

Draft:
> Your customer-success opening asks one person to own onboarding and renewals. I prepared a short example of how those two outcomes could become interview evidence and a first-month onboarding plan. Would that be useful to review?

Replace the statement only when the source supports it. Do not invent a relationship, hiring urgency, budget or observed failure. No messages are sent by the kit.

A bounded diagnostic can be a paid first engagement, but it must be independently useful: role clarity, workflow bottlenecks, an evidence rubric and a costed service proposal. Do not require a paid audit merely to reveal the actual offer.

## The digital product business

Agentic Income sells the kit and teaches the business model. Starlight contributes reusable intelligence and workflow contracts. GenCreator can produce approved recruitment and training assets. Ana can contribute a distinctive expert module after rights, authorship and compensation are agreed.

Launch one complete SKU before a bundle of promises. The original narrow Launch Kit proposed US$149; this expanded Studio review edition proposes US$499 for one agency and up to five internal users, with editable client deliverables and no vendor subscriptions or implementation service. See the Studio product overview for current scope. A later automation edition must include tested provider-specific configurations. A later human-practice module must contain expert-reviewed exercises and teaching, not a synthetic imitation of Ana.

The product is useful without an upsell: buyers can define, cost and rehearse a service from the base kit. Every next product solves a demonstrated next problem.

## Operating economics

The calculator separates revenue from payment collection, refund assumptions, transaction fees, tooling, acquisition expense and labor at a stated internal rate. It reports contribution before company tax and general overhead not entered in the model. This is scenario arithmetic, not an earnings projection.

Capacity comes from available delivery hours after time reserved for sales, administration and recovery. Plan fewer clients than the theoretical ceiling while learning. Higher-touch work can command a higher fee but also consumes scarce practitioner time; never label a service passive because AI prepares its documents.

Use the same model for the digital product: allocate development cost, acquisition, file delivery, refunds, support and updates. A US$149 sale with 90 minutes of expert support is a materially different product from a self-serve sale.

## Validation and stop decisions

Run a proposed 30-day test after the sample and delivery are reviewable:
- Show the sample to 20 qualified practitioners; record the denominator and how each was reached.
- Seek five paid pilot kit buyers only after checkout and private delivery pass.
- Observe at least four complete the practice case; interview buyers and non-buyers about the actual blocker.
- Track support minutes per activated buyer and defects in their outputs.
- Revise the product when activation is below four of five or median support exceeds the proposed 45-minute budget. These are decision thresholds, not claimed conversion benchmarks.

For the agency service, start with two bounded clients. Record baseline and intervention coordination time for comparable milestones, first-pass acceptance, candidate experience and contribution. Stop expansion when corrections consume the apparent savings or candidate problems cannot be resolved promptly.

A useful product can fail commercially because the audience lacks client access, the niche does not buy the service, the tools consume the margin, or competent human review cannot be staffed. The experiment must distinguish these failures.
