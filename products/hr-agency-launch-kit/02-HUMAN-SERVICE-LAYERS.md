# Price the human contribution

The lower tier must still provide respectful communication, an accessible alternative and accountable review. Premium fees buy additional expertise, depth, access and delivery responsibility.

The table below is an original pricing experiment in US dollars before tax, per 30-day engagement. It is not a market benchmark or a published offer.

| Service | Illustrative fee | AI-assisted work | Included human work | Hard boundary |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Managed hiring operations | $1,500 | Brief preparation, permitted source research, outreach drafts, optional disclosed AI screening, scheduling, evidence organization and weekly updates | One intake/calibration, evidence review, exceptions and decision preparation; budget 8 hours | One role, up to 25 completed screens; client conducts selection interviews and makes all hiring decisions |
| Expert recruitment | $3,500 | The same preparation and coordination | Up to six 45-minute structured interviews, calibration, work-sample review and offer-conversation preparation; budget 16 hours total | Same role and screening cap; six human interviews, one client decision meeting |
| Leadership and onboarding partnership | $7,500 | The same preparation plus onboarding coordination and progress briefs | Expert recruitment, a 90-minute team working-agreement session, two manager/new-hire check-ins and first-month integration review; budget 30 hours total | One role and one team of up to eight; one 30-day onboarding window |

All three include a named accountable reviewer and an agreed route for candidates to request human contact. Rework beyond the agreed scope triggers a revised scope or removal of lower-priority work. Replacement guarantees, payroll, employment contracts, clinical services, retreats and round-the-clock support are excluded.

A setup fee may be appropriate for a first engagement requiring data mapping and configuration; quote its actual work separately. Do not disguise setup labor as recurring margin. The included calculator models the recurring engagement only, with setup entered as an amortized cost when applicable.

## Human time is designed

The eight-hour baseline is a budget to test: intake and calibration, review of up to 25 screen summaries, exception handling, client decisions and handoff. Track actual minutes. When the budget fails, reduce volume, simplify the workflow or change price; do not silently remove review.

Use live time where it changes the result: ambiguity about the role, evidence disagreement, candidate questions, negotiation, team friction and onboarding. Replace routine status narration with a brief containing the evidence, decision required, owner and date.

The top tier must produce additional artifacts: a reviewed working agreement, explicit collaboration and escalation preferences, and a first-month integration plan. A longer call with no additional outcome is not a service upgrade.

## Optional expert method module

A future Ana collaboration can add original teaching on attention, listening, leadership, reflective practice and team relationships. Distinguish three forms:
- Job-relevant professional practice: usable across the agency service.
- Optional personal or spiritual development: chosen separately, with no effect on hiring or employment evaluation.
- Psychotherapy or clinical work: a separate professional relationship and record system, where appropriate.

No candidate personality, mental health, emotional state or spiritual affinity is inferred from voice, facial appearance or AI interview behavior. No scientific or therapeutic claim is published without relevant evidence and expert review.

## Pricing decisions

Price from customer value, scope and cost together. A fixed engagement fee gives the client clarity; the capacity model prevents the agency from overcommitting. Test the service before offering a recurring retainer.

The digital kit and the agency service have different economics. We sell reusable materials to the practitioner. The practitioner sells scoped services to clients. Purchasing this kit does not entitle clients to Ana's time or grant a practitioner the right to teach her method under her name.
