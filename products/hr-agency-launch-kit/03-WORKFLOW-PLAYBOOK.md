# Eight operating workflows

Use these as task recipes in your existing AI workspace. They do not install connectors or run workers. Every handoff carries a client/role ID, source revision, named owner, useful output and acceptance check. Drafts remain drafts until reviewed.

## 1. Client scope and role intake

Input: approved client sources, draft job description, business outcome, compensation range supplied by the client and decision owner.

Task:
> Use only the supplied sources. Draft the client scope and role brief templates. Separate job outcomes, essential criteria and preferences. Mark missing facts as unknown. Propose observable evidence for each criterion. Return contradictions and questions for the hiring manager. Do not invent salary, credentials, urgency or a hiring commitment.

Output: completed scope, evidence-linked role brief and unresolved decisions. Hiring manager accepts the brief revision. Recovery: pause downstream work when scope or decision ownership is missing.

## 2. Company knowledge and permissions

Input: the knowledge register, current role and assigned worker permissions.

Task:
> List the permitted sources for this client and purpose. Identify stale, conflicting or unowned records. Return an index with owner, last checked date, visibility and source link. Summarize only authorized process knowledge; leave candidate and personal-development records in their restricted systems.

Output: source map and access gaps. Acceptance: source owner confirms the map; retrieval is tested with both allowed and denied records. Recovery: use the last approved source or a human answer, with its date. Never expand access to solve an empty search.

## 3. Sourcing and reviewed outreach

Input: approved role criteria, permitted source list, candidate-contact policy and draft recipient set.

Task:
> Prepare a sourcing plan and draft up to five individually grounded messages. For each, cite the job-relevant source behind the personalization. Check duplicate contact and suppression status. Separate candidate outreach from client acquisition. Return drafts and missing permissions; do not send.

Output: the outreach review CSV and message variants. Acceptance: reviewer checks recipient, channel, exact message revision, opt-out route and contact history. An approved sender performs any send, logs the provider message ID and stops the sequence on reply or opt-out. Recovery: hold the batch on source error, duplicate or uncertainty.

## 4. Optional AI screening

Input: approved questions and rubric, provider configuration, candidate notice, human alternative and a synthetic test set.

Task:
> Turn the role brief into a short, job-relevant screening outline. Explain the automated format and recording use, offer a human route, and avoid questions unrelated to the work. Map each question to an approved criterion. Produce the preview script and test checklist. Do not decide who advances.

Output: previewable script and configuration checklist. A practitioner tests the experience before invitations. Check interruptions, accent variation, silence, changed availability, requests for accommodation and candidate questions. Preserve missing evidence as unknown. Human selection remains outside the voice system.

Provider scores and fraud flags are unverified suggestions, not facts. Disable unnecessary video or behavioral inference; reject a provider configuration that cannot meet the chosen boundary. A candidate's use of the human route does not count against them.

## 5. Human interview and evidence review

Input: approved brief, consented interview notes, work sample and source identifiers.

Task:
> Fill the interview evidence template. Separate verbatim excerpts, observed work evidence and interpretation. Cite each source. Mark contradictions and missing evidence. Suggest job-relevant follow-ups. Do not infer personality, health or protected traits and do not rank or reject candidates.

Output: evidence sheet and follow-up questions. Practitioner verifies material statements against the original source. The hiring manager records the decision and rationale. Recovery: ambiguous evidence prompts clarification; a low-confidence summary never becomes a silent rejection.

## 6. Weekly decisions and meeting reduction

Input: current commitments, candidate-stage records, blockers and approved sources.

Task:
> Prepare a one-page weekly decision brief: changes, evidence, owner, due date, decision requested and consequence of delay. Flag a status meeting as a possible asynchronous update only when its actual decision can be handled that way. Preserve interviews, relationship work, conflict resolution and sensitive conversations.

Output: weekly brief and proposed calendar changes. Organizer decides whether to change a meeting. Acceptance: all open commitments have an owner and deadline; no unexplained stage movement. Recovery: stale source dates are visible and the affected decision remains open.

## 7. Offer and onboarding

Input: human hiring decision, authorized offer details and the agreed start date.

Task:
> Draft an onboarding plan and the coordination checklist from authorized terms. Separate offer preparation from the employer's legal employment offer. Include access owners, first deliverable, support contact, working agreements and check-ins. Do not invent compensation or send an offer.

Output: reviewed handoff and first-month plan. HR/employer approves all offer terms and accesses. Recovery: missing approval blocks communications and provisioning; rescheduling does not silently change compensation or contract terms.

## 8. Delivery acceptance and invoicing

Input: accepted milestone, scope revision, approved rates and financial source of truth.

Task:
> Prepare a billable-event record linked to the accepted milestone. Check the engagement, revision, amount, tax treatment supplied by finance and duplicate history. Return an invoice preparation packet and discrepancies. Do not issue, send, collect or mark payment as settled.

Output: finance-reviewed invoice preparation. Use the existing Ana HR kit for local preparation where appropriate, and the established accounting platform for official invoices and settlement. A retry must reuse the billable-event identity. Recovery: discrepancy or duplicate stops issuance and goes to finance.

## Specialist handoffs

One coordinator can route tasks to knowledge, recruiting preparation, interview evidence, client delivery, finance preparation and learning/product roles. These are work responsibilities, not a requirement to buy six agents. Start with sequential tasks and add parallel work only where inputs are independent and each output can be checked.

For every vendor action, preserve the tool/version, principal, source scope, approved revision, intended effect and returned receipt. A prompt saying “approved” is not authenticated approval. The templates document the process; the connected implementation must enforce it.
