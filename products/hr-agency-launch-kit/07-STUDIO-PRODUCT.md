# AI-native HR Agency Studio

Version 0.2.0 · English review edition · 10 September 2026

Build an HR agency with dependable operations and a human service model clients can understand and buy.

The product expands the original launch kit into six reusable services, four implementation architectures, a working configuration and offer generator, an evidence assessment tool, and a co-selling blueprint. The existing product identity remains `hr-agency-launch-kit`; Studio is this expanded edition. Nothing in this edition requires a hosted subscription to complete the practice exercises.

## The business we can build

Agentic Income sells the reusable digital product and implementation education. Ana can co-design and co-sell a practitioner edition, retain authority over her contributed method, and choose whether she provides any live delivery. Other agencies use the system to sell services to their own clients. Starlight supplies the proposed managed software layer after repeated deployments demonstrate demand. GenCreator supplies approved creation and teaching workflows where useful.

Start with experienced recruiters and boutique HR agencies of 1–10 practitioners. Their repeat job is turning fragmented client knowledge, hiring activity, meeting notes and accepted work into reliable delivery. Their current workaround is the incumbent ATS plus documents, spreadsheets and many coordination conversations. The initial budget owner is the agency owner. Buyer demand for this edition has not been measured.

| Team working agreements | Clear decisions, communication and fewer unnecessary meetings | $1,500–$6,500 | Up to eight people / 30 days |

| People operations improvement | Two defined operating improvements with accountable owners | $1,500–$8,000 | One company / 30 days |

The exact caps, human work and exclusions are in each [service pack](services/README.md). Retreat fees exclude travel, venue, accommodation and separately scoped onsite support. Company headcount, active roles and completed screens are scope limits, not permission to reuse all company data.

## The core product promise to test

An experienced practitioner can produce a reviewable, costed client service and a first accepted operating artifact using the kit. Test against the same brief using their current process. Record active work minutes, material corrections, accepted artifacts, provider spend and support time. The product should improve accepted work per paid hour; artifact volume is not success.
