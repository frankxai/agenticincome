# Autonomy, assurance and human service

Use three independent dimensions when designing and pricing an agency service. A high automation level can coexist with excellent human care. More human hours can still produce poor work. Quality needs observed evidence.

## Autonomy describes permission and operating capability

| Level | Operating model | Useful example | Evidence needed before real execution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A0 Manual | Human performs the work | Resolve an employment decision or a critical incident | Named accountable person |
| A1 Prepared | AI reads authorized sources and drafts | Weekly client brief, invoice draft, interview evidence | Source scope and reviewer |
| A2 Supervised | Each external change has human approval | Create an onboarding task from an approved plan | Authenticated scope, exact payload, current version, receipt and recovery |
| A3 Bounded | Approved routine work runs inside limits | Update allowed task fields or send one approved message batch | A2 controls plus caps, expiry, exceptions and tested readback |
| A4 Managed | Bounded workflows coordinate across systems | Maintain task progress, reconcile confirmations, prepare the next client decision | A3 controls plus durable recovery, monitoring, independent review and live evidence |

These are product-specific operating levels, not an industry accreditation. A4 is not unrestricted agency authority. Apply levels per workflow, tenant and version. Hiring decisions, offers, pay changes, financial commitments, clinical work and live facilitation keep their named human authority. Routine administration is where higher autonomy starts.

Preauthorization can cover a bounded class of actions: tenant, workspace, allowed fields, exact audience/message where relevant, maximum count, budget, dates, reviewer and stop conditions. It cannot be created by a model writing “approved.” Changed recipients, role criteria, prices or supplier terms reopen the affected review. The included tool evaluates a proposed operating level; it cannot authenticate an approval or execute a provider action.

## Quality describes evidence about work

| Level | Meaning in this edition | Minimum submitted evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q0 Unverified | No supported assurance claim | No matching current sample, or unresolved material error |
| Q1 Reviewed sample | Someone reviewed usable work | At least one fully accepted case with no material error |
| Q2 Independently benchmarked sample | Independent review on a bounded task | At least 20 cases; at least 95% accepted; zero material errors; reviewer independent |
| Q3 Benchmark plus monitored live sample | Same task also observed in service | Q2 plus at least 30 live cases; at least 95% accepted; zero material errors; independent review |

Records must match service scope and workflow version and be checked within 30 days. A matching critical incident blocks progression even when old. Current material errors reset the level until corrected and reevaluated. These thresholds are pilot policy, not scientific proof of unbiased hiring, causal business impact or legal compliance. An independently reviewed fictional benchmark can support Q2 evidence but cannot establish Q3. The tool checks supplied records; evidence authenticity and claimed reviewer independence need external verification.

Report the sample, denominator, task, review date, language, role context and failure definitions beside a level. Do not pool dissimilar workflows or count the same cases repeatedly. Q3 on invoice preparation does not grade candidate screening. A new model, prompt, connector permission or material client criterion triggers a scoped regression evaluation. Do not rank people with these levels.

## Human service describes what clients receive

H1 Managed preparation includes an accountable professional reviewing defined outputs. H2 Expert delivery adds scoped interviewing, calibration, workshops or specialist intervention. H3 Embedded partnership adds deeper facilitation, individual access and implementation follow-through. Each service pack specifies the actual sessions and hours.

The baseline in every service includes respectful communication, accessibility handling and a named human route. Premium fees buy additional expertise, ownership, depth and deliverables. Quality claims are earned through evidence. A contract may set a higher quality target and price the review effort needed; it must show the current evidence separately.

## Promotion and demotion

Start A1/Q0 with fictional practice. Review a complete case before using real data. Establish A2 controls and validate one reversible write in a test tenant. Reach Q2 with independent task review before proposing A3. Move to A4 only after Q3 evidence, recoverable operations and a named maintained service exist. A failed isolation check, unapproved action, harmful decision error or unknown write outcome pauses the affected workflow immediately; successful unrelated tasks cannot average it away.

The configuration engine implements these caps as a planning assessment. All generated workflow entries set `executionEnabled: false`. The architecture activation contract describes the separate work required to enable a real service.
