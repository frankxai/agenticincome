# Co-selling and ownership blueprint

Commercial proposal for review. No agreement, revenue split, exclusivity, trademark permission or commitment of Ana's time is created by this document.

## Three businesses with clear owners

1. The digital product seller distributes Agency Studio through Agentic Income and owns checkout, receipts, file delivery, product support and refunds.
2. Ana can contribute a named practitioner module, review the craft standard, co-teach an asynchronous demonstration and refer suitable agencies. Her authorship and delivery role are chosen by her.
3. Each adopting agency contracts with its own clients and owns practitioner competence, customer data, hiring decisions, service acceptance and local delivery obligations.

Starlight is the proposed maintained software layer; GenCreator can produce licensed lessons and approved marketing assets. These are responsibilities, not a new legal entity or a common private-data pool.

## Commercial modes to choose between

| Mode | What happens | Proposed compensation basis | Must be settled before sale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Referral | Ana or another partner introduces a qualified buyer | Illustrative 20% of attributed net product receipts | Attribution window, exclusions, refunds, self-referrals, payout timing |
| Co-authored module | A practitioner contributes approved curriculum | Illustrative 25% of that module's net receipts | Exact assets, license scope, credit, revisions, withdrawal and maintenance |
| Agency implementation | A deployment partner installs and hands over a scoped client configuration | Partner quotes implementation and support separately | Acceptance, access, support boundaries, source-kit license and liability |
| Human delivery | Ana or another practitioner delivers sessions for a client | Agreed delivery fee or separately defined contribution split | Actual hours, travel, qualifications, client contract and cancellation |

Do not silently stack a referral fee and an author royalty. The settlement schedule must specify whether both apply to one purchase, their separate bases, and which party pays support. Net product receipts should be explicitly defined as collected product receipts excluding tax, less refunds/chargebacks and merchant/payment fees; do not call that profit. Keep service revenue and digital-product revenue separate.

## Illustrative Studio economics

One $499 sale, before tax. These are assumptions to test:

| --- | ---: |
| Product price | $499.00 |
| Refund/merchant/processing allowance at 7% | −$34.93 |
| Net receipts used for this example | $464.07 |
| Acquisition allowance | −$80.00 |
| Support: 90 minutes at $50/hour | −$75.00 |
| Maintenance allocation | −$40.00 |
| Delivery allowance | −$5.00 |
| Contribution before development and general overhead | $264.07 |
| Optional 20% referral fee on net receipts | −$92.81 |
| Contribution with that referral fee | $171.26 |

At three support hours and $160 acquisition cost, the same referred sale contributes about $16.26 before development and general overhead. The business depends on reusable delivery and support discipline. No buyer acquisition cost, conversion rate or margin has been measured yet.

## IP schedule to complete with Ana

- Pre-existing property: author, artifact/version, owner, source rights and permitted uses.
- New contribution: exact lesson/template/example, named author, review state and compensation.
- License: channel, territory, term, adaptation, translation, sublicensing and source-code treatment.
- Attribution: agreed name/bio, approved credentials, approved claims and use of likeness.
- Maintenance: who handles defects, new editions, research changes and practitioner review.
- Customer continuity: what purchasers retain if a partnership or license ends.
- Privacy: no candidate records, clinical notes or identifiable client stories enter training or marketing without appropriate rights and authorization.
- Commercial records: statements, refunds, currency conversion, payout schedule and dispute route.

The existing Retreat Studio strategy assigns roles for that particular venture. Do not copy those assignments into this HR product as if Ana approved them. This product lets her choose author, reviewer, facilitator, agency owner or a combination.

## Co-launch sequence with minimal founder labor

Prepare one 20-minute recorded fictional engagement walkthrough, one before/after artifact comparison, one annotated client offer and one clear product page. Run a short review session with Ana to resolve her scope and ownership preferences; asynchronous comments are sufficient where possible. No live launch date is needed to test the product.

Then invite a bounded group of experienced practitioners through channels the business is authorized to use. This package supplies message drafts; no messages are sent by the tool. After the commerce path is tested, run five paying agency pilots. Record support minutes and accepted outcomes. Turn repeated questions into product improvements before adding recurring office hours or unlimited support.

No testimonials, earnings examples or Ana endorsements should be published as facts unless they exist and are approved for that use.
