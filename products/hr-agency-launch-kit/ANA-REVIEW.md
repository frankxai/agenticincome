# Ana, a product we could build and sell together

This English review edition brings your HR agency work and our retreat work into one product family: **AI-native HR Agency Studio**.

The idea is to make the repeatable work easier—knowledge, preparation, coordination, follow-up and billing—so people can spend more attention on hiring conversations, leadership, team relationships and meaningful work. Other agencies could buy the tools and templates, then sell their own clearly scoped services.

## What is ready to review

- Six service packs covering agency operations, hiring, onboarding, team agreements, retreats and people operations.
- A working tool that generates a client offer, human delivery scope, cost model and proposed automation level.
- A quality-evidence framework that distinguishes a draft, reviewed work, independent task evaluation and observed live delivery.
- Original session-card drafts for listening, decisions, team agreements, meaning and integration.
- A proposed co-selling and ownership model, with a full digital product price hypothesis of $499 before tax.

The software currently runs locally. Provider accounts are not connected, checkout is closed and customer outcomes have not been measured. The tool does not make hiring decisions or book retreats.

## The part only you can shape

You can choose to be the method author, reviewer, facilitator, agency owner, or a combination. The product should not depend on you attending every client meeting. A recorded demonstration, annotated examples and clear practitioner standards can carry much of your contribution into reusable delivery.

Your understanding of people can shape how the work feels: better listening, clearer expectations, room for reflection and care in difficult conversations. Any optional spiritual practice stays voluntary. Clinical work remains a separate professional relationship. We would agree the exact language, rights and evidence before using your name in a commercial offer.

## Five review decisions

1. Which first service best represents work you already deliver well: hiring, onboarding, team agreements or retreat follow-through?
2. Which exercises in the human-practice module feel useful, and which would you replace?
3. What do you want to author or review, and what live delivery would you actually enjoy offering?
4. What would make you reject a client deliverable as insufficient quality?
5. What ownership, compensation and use of your name would feel right for a co-selling arrangement?

Comments are enough; these are review prompts, not a meeting request or a signed agreement. No pricing, revenue split or delivery commitment is assumed on your behalf.
