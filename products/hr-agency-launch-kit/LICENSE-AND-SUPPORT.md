# License and support proposal

Version 0.2.0 is a private review edition. No purchase or commercial activation has occurred.

The expanded Agency Studio price hypothesis is $499 once before applicable tax. The earlier $149 proposal applied to the narrower 0.1.0 Launch Kit. No existing buyer is being migrated or charged.

Proposed Studio terms:

- One purchasing agency and up to five internal users.
- Adapt original templates into deliverables sold to that agency’s clients.
- Run the included local software for that agency’s work.
- Do not resell the archive, distribute its source as another product or claim certification or endorsement from Ana.
- A separate deployment-partner license may later authorize additional agency installations; this edition grants no such rights.
- Third-party software and open-source components retain their own terms. No exclusive rights to public code are claimed.
- No rights to Ana’s unpublished method, name, likeness or private material are granted by this draft.

Proposed support: written installation and template questions for 30 days and correction of reproducible defects in the purchased release. Internal planning allowance: 90 support minutes per activated Studio buyer; not an entitlement to consulting. No client service delivery, account administration, emergency support, live coaching, response-time guarantee or perpetual updates are included.

Use the catalog’s existing 30-day one-time refund policy, subject to approved merchant terms. Before sale the owner must finalize the license, support destination, tax display and refund process, upload the private archive, and complete a real test of purchase, receipt, download and refund. Checkout remains closed until those steps and applicable production approval are complete.

A co-authored Ana module needs its own rights and compensation schedule. Referral fees, royalties and services must identify their actual bases and support responsibilities. The core Studio practice exercises are complete without buying an add-on or hiring Ana.
