# Fictional first-client offer

Cedar Lane Software · One customer-success role · 30-day hiring operations engagement

The deliverables are an approved hiring brief, coordination for up to 25 completed screens, reviewed evidence for the hiring manager, a weekly written decision brief and an onboarding handoff after a selection.

The employer conducts selection interviews and makes the hiring decision. The agency provides a named review owner and a human contact route for candidates. Optional AI screening is disclosed and previewed before invitation.

Illustrative fee: US$1,500 before applicable tax, with one revision cycle. Client ATS and office accounts remain client-owned. Additional role scope, candidate volume, human interviews or a new delivery period requires a revised agreement.

Milestone acceptance:
- Brief: hiring manager accepts the criteria and evidence plan.
- Evidence: reviewer checks the prepared packet against authorized sources.
- Completion: hiring manager receives the decision/onboarding handoff, or a closure report when the client pauses or no hire is selected.

This is a teaching example, not a binding proposal or a claim that this fee has been sold.
