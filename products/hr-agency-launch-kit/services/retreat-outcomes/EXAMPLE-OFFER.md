# Retreat planning and outcome delivery

Review draft · retreat-outcomes-practice · Product 0.2.0

## Scope and fee

One team, 8–20 participants, up to two program days. Travel, venue, food and accommodation excluded.

Fee hypothesis: USD 4,500 before tax per One event plus 30-day follow-through. Travel, venue, specialist subscriptions and unscoped human delivery excluded.

## Included outcomes

- Outcome brief, comparable supplier assumptions and readiness packet
- Timed runbook with named decision owners and participation alternatives
- Reconciled service costs and accepted day-7/day-30 action handoff

## Human service

Adds one facilitated program day; named onsite host remains separately scoped.

Budget: 32 hours including entered quality review. Additional work requires a revised scope.

## Automation and quality

Retreat and team studio; requested A3; proposed A1. Planning assessment only. No authenticated permission, live connection or execution is created.

Target Q2; submitted evidence Q0 (Unverified). Self-supplied records; authenticity and reviewer independence are not verified by this tool.

Compare every commitment to scope and receipt; track accepted actions due by day 30, not attendance alone.

## Acceptance and review

Client and practitioner agree the artifact rubric before work starts. Record acceptance, corrections, active minutes, cost and due outcomes. No hire, health, revenue or retention result is guaranteed. Candidate human contact and participation alternatives remain available.
