# Team working agreements

Review draft · team-practice-practice · Product 0.2.0

## Scope and fee

One team of up to eight people. Workplace facilitation; no clinical service.

Fee hypothesis: USD 3,500 before tax per 30-day team engagement. Travel, venue, specialist subscriptions and unscoped human delivery excluded.

## Included outcomes

- Team-approved decision and communication agreements
- One implemented meeting experiment with a baseline
- 30-day review of agreements used and work completed

## Human service

Adds a 90-minute facilitated session and two team check-ins.

Budget: 20 hours including entered quality review. Additional work requires a revised scope.

## Automation and quality

Connected agency; requested A3; proposed A1. Planning assessment only. No authenticated permission, live connection or execution is created.

Target Q2; submitted evidence Q0 (Unverified). Self-supplied records; authenticity and reviewer independence are not verified by this tool.

Every agreement needs an owner and review date; private reflection never becomes an employee score.

## Acceptance and review

Client and practitioner agree the artifact rubric before work starts. Record acceptance, corrections, active minutes, cost and due outcomes. No hire, health, revenue or retention result is guaranteed. Candidate human contact and participation alternatives remain available.

## Decisions before sending

- Unverified or stale architecture controls: tenantIsolation, sourcePermissions, authenticatedApprovals, idempotency, providerReadback, budgetLimits, killSwitch, retentionOwner
- Requested A3; evidence and architecture currently support at most proposed A1.
- Target Q2 is not yet supported; current submitted evidence is Q0.
- Name contracting parties, delivery owner, support route, currency/tax handling and payment schedule.
- Agree confidentiality, retention, change control, cancellation and remedies.
- A human must approve this client-specific offer before it is sent.
