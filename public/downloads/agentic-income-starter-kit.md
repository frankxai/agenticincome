# Agentic Income Outcome Pack Starter

Use this kit to shape the first serious business test:

1. one buyer you understand and can reach;
2. one painful workflow with evidence;
3. one accepted outcome and explicit non-goals;
4. one human and agent authority boundary;
5. one reasoned validation, revise, or stop decision.

## The 30-Minute Sprint

### 00-05: Choose The Buyer

Name a person or team you understand and can realistically reach. Avoid “everyone,” “creators,” or “small business” without a narrower context.

### 05-12: Capture The Job

Write the current process, pain, urgency, constraints, and evidence that the problem exists. Include what the buyer has already tried.

### 12-20: Define Acceptance

State the smallest valuable outcome, how it will be checked, and what is explicitly out of scope. Replace “improve,” “optimize,” and “transform” with observable conditions.

### 20-25: Map Authority

Separate agent preparation from human approval. Pricing, contracts, customer-facing sends, production promotion, credential grants, public income claims, and movement of money require an authorized principal.

### 25-30: Make The Decision

Choose one next validation action and define a revise-or-stop condition before building. Examples: three qualified buyer conversations, one paid pilot, or one accepted manual delivery by a specific date.

## First Loop Checklist

- [ ] Buyer and painful job named
- [ ] Evidence and current process recorded
- [ ] Accepted outcome and non-goals written
- [ ] Legal or human principal named
- [ ] Agent authority and human gates mapped
- [ ] Next validation action chosen
- [ ] Revise-or-stop condition written
- [ ] Credentials and customer data excluded from Git and model context

## Outcome Pack Rule

Agents prepare. An authorized principal commits. The buyer, offer, workflow, authority, evaluations, economics, evidence, and runbook must be inspectable before the system is described as ready.
