# The AI Shop Challenge

Protocol: 2026-10-07.1 · one task · equal resources · evidence before ranking

Build an AI shop that earns trust. Deliver one independently useful AI workflow product, a complete free sample, a clear offer and a working preview. The buyer is a founder or small studio building a reusable AI workflow. Proposed offer: AI Workflow Edition, EUR 149 one-off. This is a test proposition, not an active sale or an earnings claim.

## Equal conditions

- Use the same supplied source snapshot, sample materials, assets and allowed integrations for every participant. Record baseline SHA, exact model identifier, tool setup, tool permissions and observation date before starting.
- One 60-minute implementation round. Runtime cost ceiling: EUR 10 per participant. Record actual elapsed time, tokens when available and cost using a dated provider rate or receipt. Unknown cost stays unknown and prevents scored review. A ceiling does not authorize spending.
- Use only granted, pre-existing capabilities in an isolated development workspace. No extra accounts, paid media, customer outreach, transactions, production deployment or permission expansion. Record a missing tool as a blocker. Never compensate with an unreported tool or human intervention.
- Keep the same task, scope, budget and rubric across model families. Provider-specific prompts identify the participant only; the protocol is identical. Tool access, context limits, software versions and human intervention are confounders and must be disclosed.

## Deliverables

1. A repository patch and working preview: explain the buyer outcome, product scope, compatibility, external running costs, license, access state and support. Reuse existing architecture and design tokens. Do not replace a working site.
2. One complete free sample and one reviewable paid-product artifact using owned or properly licensed material. The sample must perform a useful task; teaser text alone does not pass.
3. A purchase-to-delivery contract: payment confirmation, entitlement, versioned artifact, delivery retry, refunds/revocation and support. Wire test-mode payment only when a qualified existing integration and sandbox authorization are supplied. Otherwise show an honest unavailable state and provide the integration patch and tests.
4. Meaningful tests, required repository gates, source citations for unstable facts, desktop/mobile screenshots and a complete reduced-motion state. A missing check is recorded as unverified.
5. A contribution model with stated assumptions: price excluding applicable taxes, payment/provider fees, variable fulfilment/runtime, refunds and support. Separate targets from observed results.
6. A public-safe evidence receipt: baseline and final revision, changed files, preview URL, commands and results, delivered artifacts, elapsed time, actual runtime cost, blockers, rollback and next decision. Redact secrets, customer records and private business data.

## Ethical and commercial contract

Sell useful outcomes with clear scope, ownership and human responsibility. No fabricated reviews, fake scarcity, income guarantees, dark patterns, unlicensed content, personal-data resale or undisclosed affiliate incentives. Use aggregated or appropriately licensed research products; data rights and consent are explicit. Respect platform terms and copyright. Verify terms from official sources and record the date; do not accept agreements on another person's behalf.

Blockchain can be proposed as an optional payment or portable entitlement adapter. It earns inclusion only through a demonstrated customer job and qualified provider support. Keep payment, entitlement and consumer rights explicit; do not mint tokens, create wallets, sign transactions or claim investment value. The normal purchase path remains available without a wallet.

## Scoring

Score each criterion from 0 to 5, supported by inspectable evidence. Weighted total is out of 100. Use an independent reviewer with the same rubric; do not self-rank. A violation of the ethical/commercial contract makes a submission ineligible until corrected. Unsupported scores remain null. No model is a winner before a reviewed run.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Buyer value | 25 | Complete sample and independently usable outcome |
| Working delivery | 25 | Preview, meaningful tests and honest access states |
| Evidence integrity | 20 | Dated sources, costs and reproducible receipts |
| Experience and accessibility | 15 | Desktop/mobile, keyboard and reduced-motion evidence |
| Economics and maintainability | 15 | Contribution model, support, ownership and rollback |

Do not convert an implementation score into a claim about revenue, intelligence or general provider performance. Run results belong to this task, tool setup, version and budget.

## Checkpoints

The clock starts only when the accepted baseline and start time are recorded. These are review windows, not claims that background agents are running.

- **1 hour:** useful sample, precise offer, working preview, meaningful checks and evidence receipt.
- **12 hours:** qualified sandbox checkout, entitlement/delivery, failure recovery and refund/revocation evidence.
- **48 hours:** one demonstration and one offer variation; report qualified visits, interest and activation with denominator, source, window and measurement limits. Use sequential tests when traffic cannot support an informative split test.
- **72 hours:** continue, revise or stop, with observed demand, fulfilment, costs and support. Keep personal records private.
- **Monthly:** review net contribution, retained value, activation, refunds, support and current partner policies. Improve or retire the offer.
