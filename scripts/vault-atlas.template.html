The decision Sell the map, not the commodity.

Tokens, GPUs and generations are price-transparent: routers publish vendor prices, so a markup is visible and a vendor price cut erases it. The estate sells what sits on top: sourced knowledge, BYOK skills and templates, and creations priced per outcome. Distribution goes where buyers already are, and the take rate is what that distribution costs.
