# Honest Marketing

The category screams income screenshots; we show machinery. This is simultaneously the compliance floor and the conversion strategy.

## The legal floor (never cross)

1. **Zero earnings claims** — no dollar outcomes, no "results not typical" workarounds, no lifestyle implication marketing. We market machinery and education, full stop.
2. **Disclosure before the click** — compensated links labeled inline ("commission link — selection doctrine here"), never footer-only. Material connections (payment, gifts, partnership) stated at the top of the artifact.
3. **Evidence labels on reviews** — hands-on / source-verified / desk review. Never imply hands-on that didn't happen.
4. **Attested proof only** — cases dated, method shown, consent recorded; revenue never shown without costs (knowledge/06 format).
5. **EU:** withdrawal rights via MoR; no dark patterns; label AI-generated content where its nature could mislead.

## The voice (inspiring-honest)

Pattern: **bold capability claim → exact mechanism → honest costs → one clear action.**

- Kill list: hustle, secret, unlock, effortless, 6-figure, autopilot, overnight, guru, ninja, "passive income" unqualified.
- Power list: machinery, architecture, attested, inspectable, compound, sovereign, ship, build.
- Every superlative must survive the question "compared to what, shown where?"
- Honest-costs block on every offer page: labor floor, latency to first result, failure modes. It reads like anti-marketing; it converts like proof.

## The two-question gate

Before any copy ships: **(1)** Would a skeptical senior engineer respect this? **(2)** Would the FTC yawn at it? Both yes, or rewrite.

## Distribution doctrine

Earned only: knowledge-base SEO (deep, dated, re-verified pages — also the LLM-citation layer), open-source repos as landing pages, the weekly Brief, syndication cuts linking to the canonical. No paid ads, no engagement bait, no launch theater.
