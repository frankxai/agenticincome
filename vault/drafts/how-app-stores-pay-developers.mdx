# How App Stores Pay Developers

When software builders ask how much mobile app stores take from a sale, the headline answer of thirty percent hides how the money actually moves. Both Apple and Google separate physical commerce and advertising from digital goods sold inside an app, and both apply lower tiers for smaller developers and recurring subscriptions. Every figure below comes from primary platform disclosures verified in the Economy Vault on 2026-10-25.

## The $1.4 Trillion Headline vs. the Digital Commission Base

In a study conducted by economists at Analysis Group and published by Apple on 4 June 2026, the Apple App Store ecosystem facilitated [over $1.4 trillion in developer billings and sales in calendar year 2025 (CY2025, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/). That headline number is frequently cited as proof of the scale of mobile software, yet most of that volume never touches Apple's in-app payment rail.

The same Analysis Group study breaks the [over $1.4 trillion CY2025 total (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/) into three distinct buckets:

1. Physical goods and services accounted for [$1.1 trillion in CY2025 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/). This category covers ride hailing, grocery delivery, retail shopping, travel booking, and physical services ordered through an iOS app. Apple collects no commission on physical goods and services.
2. In-app advertising generated [$151 billion in CY2025 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/), where developers monetize attention through ad networks rather than charging the user at checkout. Apple does not levy an App Store commission on third-party ad revenue inside apps.
3. Digital goods and services reached [$149 billion in CY2025 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/). This is the specific slice where Apple acts as merchant of record for in-app purchases, paid downloads, and digital subscriptions.

Because physical commerce and advertising sit outside the in-app billing rail, Apple states in its [4 June 2026 release covering CY2025 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/) that developers paid no commission to Apple on more than [90% of the billings and sales facilitated by the App Store ecosystem in CY2025 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/). For an independent software builder or an agent-assisted studio selling digital software, however, the relevant pool is the [$149 billion digital goods and services segment in CY2025 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/).

## How Apple's Digital Commission Tiers Work

Within that [$149 billion digital goods and services economy in CY2025 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/), Apple applies two main commission rates depending on developer scale:

- The standard commission rate on digital goods and services is [30% for CY2025 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/).
- Under the App Store Small Business Program, developers who earned up to [$1 million in proceeds during the prior calendar year pay a 15% commission rate (CY2025, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/).

For most independent software teams below the [$1 million annual threshold (CY2025, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/), the effective App Store take rate on digital transactions is [15% rather than 30% (CY2025, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/), provided the developer enrolls in the Small Business Program and remains under the cap.

## How Google Play Structures Its Service Fees in 2026

Google Play uses a progressive tier model rather than a cliff tied solely to prior-year eligibility, and it prices recurring subscriptions at a lower baseline rate. According to [Google Play's official service fee documentation for 2026 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en):

- Developers pay a [15% service fee on the first $1 million (USD) of earnings each calendar year (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en) once enrolled in the [15% service fee tier (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en).
- Earnings above [$1 million (USD) in a calendar year are charged the standard 30% service fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en).
- Auto-renewing subscription products carry a [15% service fee starting from day one (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en), regardless of whether the subscriber is in their first month or second year.

In addition to those global baseline tiers, [Google Play's 2026 service fee documentation (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en) details a newly unbundled fee structure rolling out across major jurisdictions during 2026. This updated structure takes effect on [30 June 2026 in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and the United States, and on 30 September 2026 in Australia and Japan (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en):

- Under the updated regional schedule, Google separates the platform service fee from the payment processing fee. Developers who choose to use Google Play Billing in the EEA, UK, and US pay a [5% billing fee on top of the service fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en), while in Australia, Japan, and the rest of the world the billing fee is set at a market-specific rate.
- Recurring subscriptions under the new regional schedule carry a [10% service fee, which combines with the 5% billing fee to equal 15% when using Google Play Billing in the EEA, UK, and US (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en).
- Other in-app digital transactions carry a [20% service fee on new app installs for developers participating in Google's Level Up or Apps Experience programs, or 25% on existing installs (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en), before adding the [5% billing fee when Google Play Billing is used (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en).
- Transactions completed on an external website after a user follows an external link from the app carry a [20% service fee, or 15% on new installs for developers in the Level Up and Apps Experience programs (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en).

## What the Take Rate Covers and What It Costs Your Unit Margin

When you sell a digital product through Apple's [$149 billion digital App Store rail (CY2025, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/) or under [Google Play's 15% to 30% service fee tiers (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en), the platform acts as merchant of record. That fee covers global card and wallet acceptance, local value-added tax and sales tax remittance, device-native biometric checkout, subscription billing retries, refund handling, and distribution inside the operating system's native store.

For software that relies on metered AI inference, however, a [15% or 30% platform take rate (CY2025 and 2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en) comes directly off top-line gross billings before you pay a single cent for model tokens, voice synthesis, or image generation. If your application bundles heavy inference usage inside a low monthly price, the combination of a [15% to 30% store fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en) and variable API costs can compress gross margins rapidly whenever a power user exceeds average consumption.

## Architectural Takeaways for Builders

Before choosing between native mobile in-app billing and direct web distribution, map your unit economics against the verified platform rules:

1. Check small-developer and subscription eligibility first. Enrolling in Apple's Small Business Program or Google Play's [first $1 million tier and auto-renewing subscription tier keeps the service fee at 15% instead of 30% (CY2025 and 2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en).
2. Account for unbundled billing vs. service fees in 2026. In the EEA, UK, and US after [30 June 2026 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en), switching away from Google Play Billing removes Google's [5% billing fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en), but your own payment processor fees and tax compliance costs still apply on top of the remaining [10% to 25% Google Play service fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en).
3. Cap variable inference costs inside store-billed tiers. Because the app store collects its [15% or 30% share on gross billings (CY2025 and 2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/app-store-ecosystem-reaches-1-point-4-trillion-usd-as-developers-thrive-globally/), every mobile tier that includes AI generation needs explicit usage budgets so that supplier bills never outgrow the post-commission revenue you keep.
