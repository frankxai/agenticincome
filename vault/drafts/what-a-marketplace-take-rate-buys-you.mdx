# What a Marketplace Take Rate Buys You

Every digital product builder faces the same distribution question: should you sell solely through a direct checkout link where you bring every visitor yourself, or list inside a marketplace that takes a larger cut of the sale? A take rate is not a single thing. Depending on the platform, the percentage you give up pays for merchant-of-record tax handling, payment processing, refund holding, or organic buyer discovery inside an existing catalog.

To see what those percentages actually buy, we compare the verified fee schedules of Gumroad, Notion Marketplace, and RapidAPI Hub from the Economy Vault, and run the exact unit-margin math on a hypothesis price.

## Gumroad Splits Payment Processing From Discovery

Gumroad makes the boundary between payment infrastructure and buyer discovery explicit by charging two completely different rates on the same product. According to [Gumroad's official pricing page for 2026 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing):

- When a customer buys through your own direct link or profile, Gumroad charges [10% + $0.50 per direct sale (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing), before external credit card processing or PayPal fees when applicable.
- When a new customer finds and buys your product through the Gumroad Discover marketplace recommendation engine, Gumroad charges a flat [30% on Discover marketplace sales (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing), which includes payment processing.

That spread isolates the price of customer acquisition inside Gumroad's ecosystem. On a direct sale, the [10% + $0.50 fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing) pays for hosted checkout, file delivery, license keys, and merchant-of-record sales tax remittance. You supply the traffic. On a Discover sale, the gap between the direct rate and the [30% Discover marketplace fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing) is a pure performance fee that you pay only when Gumroad's internal search and recommendation surfaces bring you a buyer.

## How Direct vs. Discover Changes Unit Margin on a $29 Hypothesis

The Economy Vault tracks two committed unit-margin scenarios in `vault/margins.json` for a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) creator skill pack where the buyer runs the prompts and tools on their own API key, keeping per-sale inference cost at zero. Using our committed hypothesis price of $29 (`priceIsHypothesis: true`):

- In scenario `gencreator-byok-pack-gumroad`, selling the $29 hypothesis pack via a direct link under Gumroad's [10% + $0.50 per direct sale fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing) costs $3.40 in platform fees ($2.90 percentage fee plus the [$0.50 fixed fee, 2026, retrieved 2026-10-25](https://gumroad.com/pricing)). You keep $25.60 per sale, which works out to an 88.3% gross margin on the $29 hypothesis price.
- In scenario `gencreator-byok-pack-gumroad-discover`, selling that same $29 hypothesis pack through Gumroad Discover at the [30% Discover marketplace fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing) costs $8.70 in platform fees. You keep $20.30 per sale, reducing the gross margin on the $29 hypothesis price from 88.3% to 70%.

That 18.3 percentage-point difference in gross margin on a $29 hypothesis price ($5.30 per unit) is the exact dollar cost of letting the marketplace find the customer. Because a BYOK digital download has no per-unit inference cost, a 70% gross margin on incremental buyers you would not have reached on your own remains positive on unit one, whereas paying [30% on Discover marketplace sales (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing) for traffic you already owned through your newsletter or documentation would be an unnecessary tax.

## Notion Marketplace Trades Lower Fees for Ecosystem Fit

When your product is built natively for a single host application, the host workspace often runs its own template store. According to [Notion's official Selling on Marketplace documentation for 2026 (retrieved 2026-10-25, reviewer-confirmed 2026-10-26)](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace):

- Notion charges a transaction fee of [8% + $0.40 per transaction on paid templates sold through Notion Marketplace (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25, reviewer-confirmed 2026-10-26)](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace).
- For payouts in currencies other than US dollars, Stripe adds a [1% currency conversion fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25, reviewer-confirmed 2026-10-26)](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace).
- Notion holds marketplace earnings for a [14-day period before payout to cover potential buyer refunds (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25, reviewer-confirmed 2026-10-26)](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace).

Compared with Gumroad's [10% + $0.50 direct sale fee and 30% Discover fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing), Notion's [8% + $0.40 per transaction rate (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25, reviewer-confirmed 2026-10-26)](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace) is lower than a standalone Gumroad direct link while also placing the template inside Notion's native template gallery and workspace duplication flow. What you give up in exchange is format portability: a Notion Marketplace listing can only deliver a Notion template into a buyer's Notion workspace, and payouts wait for the [14-day refund hold window (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25, reviewer-confirmed 2026-10-26)](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace).

## RapidAPI Hub Prices Metered API Distribution at 25%

For developers and agent builders selling machine-callable APIs rather than static downloads or workspace templates, the marketplace provides API key provisioning, quota enforcement, subscription tiering, and developer billing. According to [RapidAPI's official Payouts and Finance documentation (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://docs.rapidapi.com/docs/payouts-and-finance):

- RapidAPI collects a [25% marketplace fee on all payments processed through the RapidAPI Hub starting 15 November 2025 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://docs.rapidapi.com/docs/payouts-and-finance).
- Prior to [15 November 2025, RapidAPI's marketplace fee was 20% (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://docs.rapidapi.com/docs/payouts-and-finance).
- Any applicable PayPal payout fees are deducted in addition to the [25% RapidAPI Hub marketplace fee (from 2025-11-15, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://docs.rapidapi.com/docs/payouts-and-finance).

Unlike a static template or a BYOK prompt pack where unit delivery cost is near zero, an API listed on RapidAPI Hub incurs server compute, database reads, or upstream model costs every time a customer calls your endpoint. When the marketplace takes [25% of gross API billings (from 2025-11-15, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://docs.rapidapi.com/docs/payouts-and-finance) off the top, your remaining 75% share must cover all infrastructure and upstream supplier costs.

## How to Choose Your Distribution Rail

The verified fee schedules across these three platforms point to three practical rules for product architecture:

1. Separate your owned-audience checkout from discovery marketplaces. If a reader is already on your site or email list, sending them through a [30% discovery rail like Gumroad Discover (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing) forfeits margin that belongs to your own distribution. Use direct links at [10% + $0.50 on Gumroad (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://gumroad.com/pricing) or a dedicated merchant-of-record checkout for traffic you bring yourself, and treat marketplace discovery as an incremental channel.
2. Match the store to the artifact's native container. When the product is a workspace system, listing inside [Notion Marketplace at 8% + $0.40 per transaction (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25, reviewer-confirmed 2026-10-26)](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace) pairs one-click installation with a lower fee than general-purpose creator storefronts, provided you account for the [14-day payout hold and 1% non-USD conversion fee (2026, retrieved 2026-10-25, reviewer-confirmed 2026-10-26)](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace).
3. Keep high-COGS inference out of high-take-rate API hubs unless priced for it. Under RapidAPI Hub's [25% marketplace fee effective 15 November 2025 (retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://docs.rapidapi.com/docs/payouts-and-finance), endpoints that wrap expensive upstream models need strict rate limits and per-call pricing that leaves a healthy buffer after the [25% platform cut (from 2025-11-15, retrieved 2026-10-25)](https://docs.rapidapi.com/docs/payouts-and-finance).
